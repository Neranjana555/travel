The Itinerary

8-Day Luxury Tour Plan

A carefully crafted journey through the best of Sri Lanka

Day 1

Airport to Negombo

  • Airport Pickup
  • Muthurajawela Boat Safari
  • Check-in: Goddess Garden Hotel / Fresco Water Villa
Day 2

Sigiriya & Dambulla

  • Dambulla Cave Temple
  • Sigiriya Rock Fortress
  • Hiriwadunna Village Tour
  • Pidurangala Rock
  • Check-in: Goddess Garden Hotel / Fresco Water Villa
Day 3

Kandy

  • Minneriya National Park Safari
  • Matale Spice Garden & Hindu Temple
  • Temple of the Tooth & Kandy Lake
  • Kandy Cultural Dance Show
  • Check-in: Kandy Riverside Villa / Lavendish Hotel
Day 4

Nuwara Eliya

  • Royal Botanical Garden
  • Ambuluwawa Tower
  • Ramboda Falls & Labookellie Tea Factory
  • Check-in: Thuruliya Hotel / Oak Ray Tea Bush
Day 5

Ella

  • Nine Arch Bridge & Flying Ravana
  • Ella Swing & Secret Waterfall
  • Sri Lankan Cooking Class
  • Check-in: Nine Arch Lodge / Moi Ella
Day 6

Yala

  • Lipton's Seat View Point
  • Buduruwagala Temple & Tissa Wewa Lake
  • Diyaluma Falls
  • Check-in: Grand Yala Hotel / Farm Villa by Heritage
Day 7

Mirissa

  • Yala National Park Safari
  • Kirinda Temple & Beach
  • Mirissa Coconut Tree Hill
  • Check-in: Windy Garden Hotel / Mandara Resort
Day 8

Galle & Departure

  • Sea Turtle Hatchery
  • Stilt Fishermen (Koggala)
  • Galle Fort
  • Unawatuna Beach
  • End Tour
